How to Play Quoridor

Goal: Move your pawn to the opposite side of the board.

On your turn, do one of the following:

  • Move your pawn one square (up, down, left, or right). You can jump over an adjacent opponent.
  • Place a wall to block your opponents' path. Walls span two squares.

Rules:

  • You cannot completely block any player from reaching their goal — at least one path must always remain.
  • Each player has a limited number of walls (shown in the side panel).
  • The first player to reach the opposite side wins!

Controls: Click a highlighted square to move, or click between squares to place a wall.
